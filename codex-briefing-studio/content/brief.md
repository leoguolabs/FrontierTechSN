# Does AI Help You Learn—or Just Finish?

An English film for people who use AI for learning and work. The useful outcome
is a better independent attempt, assessed separately from assisted output.
This is a new, condensed treatment of the existing subject, authored independently
of FrontierTechSN Tasks and the lemo-opuscar engine.

The board is a physical argument. An answer on a photographic notebook cutout becomes an
empty sheet. Four measures lead to two scaled experimental comparisons. A
hint-based tutor and a successful physics tutor prevent a blanket anti-AI claim.
The practical sequence ends in a fresh problem. The final pull-back reveals the
entire argument and the title, which has been physically on the wall throughout.

One 6400 × 3600 enamel board, warm window light, graphite rules, blue measurements,
rare red verdicts. Photograph cutouts, paper slips, magnets, brushed ruler,
erased ghosts, imperfect marker lettering, shared light direction and shallow
parallax. Continuous camera at 24 fps, held judgement frames, final wall reveal.
Main delivery: 1920 × 1080 H.264, English Chatterbox voice, subtitles, original
small-ensemble score and event-bound foley. Duration follows natural speech.
