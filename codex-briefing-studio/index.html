04 / FIELD NOTES

Read the published article on X ↗

What it takes
to match a style.

The board has a memory. Every measurement, erased line, and pinned object stays somewhere in the scene.

That observation shaped this build. A reference film became a set of physical and editorial rules: camera movement follows reasoning, annotations appear in sequence, and a final pullback reveals the argument as a whole.

This project applies those rules to a different question: whether AI helps people learn, or makes the assisted session look better. The film keeps those outcomes separate.
