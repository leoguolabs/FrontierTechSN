## Differences from the reference

The final film lasts 720.166667 seconds; the reference lasts 111 seconds.
Research qualifications, arithmetic and narration require longer holds.
The long film's twelve-chapter board is denser and more regular than the
reference composition. Fine writing is deliberately small in the final
overview; reading occurs in the preceding close views. The added people and
red flag also change the distribution of marks and colors.

These are material stylistic differences. The review supports a close
recreation of the whiteboard medium and motion vocabulary, not identical
content, shot timing or overall effect.
